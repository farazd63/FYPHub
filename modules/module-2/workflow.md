# Module 2 workflow

Registration validates roster records, configured group size and cycle membership before creating a shared team account. Roster eligibility alone does not prove each initial member's individual consent.

A student may submit a join request with their roster email OTP bound to the team. The recorded creator verifies their own roster email for the exact accept/reject action. The creator may also send an invitation after their own roster OTP; the invitee verifies their own roster email for the invitation decision. Acceptance checks capacity and duplicate membership. Live email delivery and browser execution have not been verified in this audit.

Teams can enter eight optional preference fields across six topics and opt in to deterministic same-cycle team suggestions. Separately, a roster-verified individual can submit eight answers for a one-time comparison with opted-in teams in the same cycle. Results expose only team IDs and comparable category outcomes; answers are not saved. This deterministic discovery is not AI teammate matching. A qualifying team pair can use temporary match chat. Closure or opt-out revokes access; a new conversation after closure requires fresh consent from both teams.

The shared workspace provides the seven named message channels and a group chatbot integration. The chatbot needs a configured local runtime; live operation has not been verified here.
