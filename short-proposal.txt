Introduction

    FYPHub is a web-based, AI-assisted platform designed to manage the complete univer-
sity Final Year Project (FYP) lifecycle in one role-aware system. It connects team formation,
proposal submission, university-format compliance, originality analysis, supervisor allocation,
proposal review, coordinator routing, defense scheduling, panel evaluation, progress monitoring,
final archiving, and institutional knowledge reuse. The platform combines deterministic aca-
demic rules with Large Language Models (LLMs), semantic embeddings, Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG), GraphRAG, optimization, and risk intelligence while keeping final academic
authority with authorized university personnel.

Purpose

    The purpose of FYPHub is to reduce fragmented and manual FYP processes, improve pro-
posal quality, prevent unnecessary topic duplication, make supervisor allocation more transpar-
ent, preserve review and evaluation history, and convert completed FYP records into reusable
institutional knowledge. It also supports students who do not yet have a complete team through
AI-based teammate matching and prevents duplicate membership in more than one active FYP
group during the same cycle.

Targeted Audience

    FYPHub is intended for five major roles: Students, Supervisors, FYP Coordinators,
Panel Members, and Administrators. Each role accesses the same shared modules through
role-based permissions rather than having duplicate modules for the same functionality.

Product Functions and Features

    The system contains twelve integrated modules: Admin Dashboard & Academic Man-
agement for users, roles, academic policies, templates, deadlines, rubrics, and audit history;
Student Team & Collaboration Hub for group creation, duplicate-member prevention, AI
teammate matching, join requests, seven collaboration channels, and a group chatbot; Stu-
dent Proposal & AI Compliance Check for short/long proposal submission, versioning,
university-format validation, structural checks, and academic consistency analysis; Proposal
Similarity & Originality Check for semantic comparison with archived FYPs, duplication
evidence, contribution boundaries, and extension gaps; Supervisor Selection & Alloca-
tion for expertise matching, ranking, slot/capacity management, requests, and fair assignment;
Supervisor Proposal Review & Revision for comments, editing suggestions, revision re-
quests, two-way discussion, version comparison, and AI revision-compliance checking; Coor-
dinator Proposal Control for hold, return, forward-to-panel decisions, delay detection, and
version-aware academic history; Defense & Panel Scheduling for panel assignment, avail-
ability, date/time/room scheduling, conflict detection, and rescheduling; Panel Evaluation
& Feedback for rubric scoring, detailed comments, AI-generated viva questions, evaluator-
disagreement detection, and feedback resolution; Project Progress & Supervision Dash-
board for supervision meetings, AI action extraction, milestones, project-health classification,
risk explanation, and recovery recommendations; Final FYP Archive & Handover for fi-
nal reports, code/data metadata, implemented-scope analysis, limitations, future work, and
continuation packages; and FYP Knowledge Search & Analytics for natural-language in-
stitutional search, RAG/GraphRAG, multi-hop reasoning, evidence citations, and institutional
FYP trends.

Social and Industrial Impact

    FYPHub strengthens institutional knowledge continuity by preserving the FYP journey
from team formation to final handover. It can reduce repeated project ideas, improve proposal
compliance and review transparency, support fair supervisor allocation, reduce scheduling con-
flicts, surface project risks, and make previous FYP knowledge searchable for future batches.
The project also demonstrates responsible use of AI, semantic search, optimization, analytics,
and human-in-the-loop decision support in an academic management environment.
